## Findings by hub

### GTA Online

The portal, five focused routes, goal runway and playbooks already form the strongest general decision loop. The new stable weekly address adds the recurring reason to return that the root portal lacked. The current ceiling is not another business card: it is a complete portfolio model for Nightclub warehouse dependencies, upgrades, capital already owned and incremental payoff.

### Dota 2

The item atlas and build planner provide the deepest data surface in the product. Patch and sample boundaries are visible, and item pages have stable URLs. The next valuable gap is player-specific conversion: four replay timestamps, reliable liquidity before an objective and a direct comparison between planned and realized item timing. No new generic item tier list is needed.

### WoW Retail

The hub already separated listed value from cash and connected gathering, crafting and orders. This sprint adds the missing batch-control layer: protected reserve, deployment ceiling, first-cycle cash recovery and inventory remaining at cost. Live regional prices and WoW Token context remain blocked until a supported, reviewable Blizzard data workflow exists.

### Total War: Warhammer III

The campaign-capital model is coherent, but the three generic models must become more recognizably useful inside actual campaigns. The next sprint should add a small set of faction/phase presets only where current rules can be sourced and all economic values remain editable. The September 24 release stays a review trigger, not a source of live formulas.

### Crusader Kings III

The dynasty-capital framing is differentiated, especially the succession buffer. The missing layer is a connected state sheet that carries domain income, war burn and heir reserve through one horizon. Silk & Silver remains outside current calculations until its mechanics are live and verified.

## Sequenced work, not a freeze

1. Current sprint: WoW inventory turn, GTA weekly utility and acquisition measurement.
2. Next deep sprint: Total War campaign presets and a faction/phase field guide.
3. Then: Crusader Kings dynasty runway connecting domain, war and succession.
4. Then: Dota replay-economy capture linked to item timings.
5. GTA remains on a weekly editorial cadence throughout; the Nightclub portfolio layer enters when its input contract is complete.

All five hubs remain live. A hub that is not the weekly deep focus still receives freshness checks, working links, shared measurement and correction of factual errors.
