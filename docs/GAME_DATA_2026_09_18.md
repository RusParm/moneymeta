## Implementation and limitations

- Current item constants use an explicit Valve delta over the saved OpenDota catalog. Seven price overrides include unchanged Hydra total. Attributes and displayed ability text are updated for the patch's represented changes. Heart's archived 5100 and Silver Edge's archived 25% already disagreed with Valve's stated previous values; current absolute values (5300 and 20%) win.
- The raw 7.41e snapshot, hash, match counts, dates and quartiles are unchanged. All item calculators display current-price versus historical-timing provenance, including compact views. Saved contexts distinguish the new prices without making old observations fresh.
- The archived collector is bounded before the 7.41f release day. It must not collect later games under the old label. Exact release hour is unavailable; exclusion of the whole day is conservative. Draft profiles and match timing benchmarks are not certified for 7.41f; new-match draft claims remain hidden pending a separate profile review. No fabricated new cohort.
- GTA uses research, Ammu-Nation and Enhanced Community Series as its three modeled routes. Safeguard's bonus is included in weekly context. Research progress and RP are not priced as cash. A one-time million needs explicit eligibility and all three cycles in the proposed plan; previous progress is not modeled. The checkbox defaults off and excludes rewards already earned. Model time must include waits. Only Grapeseed Bunker is free; no production-business cost or sale multiplier is globally changed.
- Weekly form drafts and links are bound to a week. Old route inputs cannot silently become new research-mission assumptions. The existing eight-week immutable outcome journal remains intact; the new optional eligibility field is backward compatible.
- WoW notes distinguish restored resources from unlimited supply and Corrosive Coin from gold. AH fees, market prices, sell-through, margins and business rankings are not recertified by news dates.
