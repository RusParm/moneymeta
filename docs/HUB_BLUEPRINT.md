# Money Meta: living game hub blueprint

## Product standard

Every game hub is a standalone decision product, not a category page with links. A player should be able to answer three questions without leaving it:

1. How does this economy move value?
2. What should a player like me do next?
3. What changed, and how confident is Money Meta in the answer?

The visual character is a deliberate hybrid: cinematic game-world immersion sets the context; product UI, real calculations and visible provenance deliver the value.

## Shared hub anatomy

### 1. World and promise

- Original Money Meta hero world with a distinct economy metaphor.
- One game-specific promise and one primary action.
- A compact proof strip showing the questions and models already covered.
- No publisher art is required for the hub to feel complete.

### 2. Visual economy map

- Interactive HTML/SVG, never a static infographic with baked-in numbers.
- Shows where value enters, compounds, gets delayed, becomes liquid and is reinvested.
- Each node opens a short explanation, a current metric and a relevant tool or analysis.
- Desktop uses a connected map; mobile uses the same nodes as a readable vertical flow.

### 3. Personal player path

- Returner: what changed and the safest re-entry sequence.
- Solo/casual: highest value per limited active hour with a friction ceiling.
- Advanced grinder: deeper optimization, edge cases and sensitivity analysis.
- Inputs persist locally and can be shared by URL before accounts exist.

### 4. Current pulse

- Patch, season or weekly context with a visible checked date.
- `verified`, `estimated`, `community signal` and `stale` states are never visually interchangeable.
- Stale information automatically loses its “current” treatment.
- Every change answers “what should I do differently?” rather than only repeating patch notes.

### 5. Decision deck

- Three to six high-intent questions above the large library.
- Every card leads to a calculation, comparison, route or analysis.
- The deck adapts to the hub’s economy instead of using generic guide categories.

### 6. Rankings and comparisons

- Rankings are always conditional: goal, budget, time, play style and risk.
- Score composition and editable assumptions are visible.
- A tier or rank without an explanation and freshness label is not publishable.
- Tables have a compact mobile alternative instead of forced horizontal reading.

### 7. Calculator and scenario lab

- Results lead with a decision, followed by the model and sensitivity.
- Show the primary recommendation, alternatives and the condition that flips the answer.
- Save, reset and share are part of every mature model.
- Calculators do not invent precision where the source data is estimated.

### 8. Research library

- Flagship analyses, evergreen explainers, comparisons, returner guides and update briefs.
- Each item displays game version, checked date, reading time and evidence status.
- Articles always complete a loop into a tool or next decision.
- Library filters should eventually include player type, goal, system and freshness.

### 9. Trust rail

- Method, sources, assumptions, changelog and correction path remain close to the result.
- Publisher independence is explicit without visually dominating the experience.
- No founder identity, personal profile or personal data is used as a trust shortcut.

## Vertical definitions

### GTA Online: portfolio economy

**Map:** starting capital → acquisition → supply → production → storage → sale → reinvestment.

**Player paths:** returner rebuild, solo two-hour route, low-friction passive portfolio, advanced weekly allocation.

**Pulse:** weekly bonuses, discounts and event effects with an automatic freshness guard.

**Rankings:** first purchase, solo efficiency, low-friction income, production return, active cash, portfolio role.

**Tool stack:** Next Best Move, business ROI, portfolio optimizer, weekly opportunity cost, goal planner and GTA VI Economy Watch based only on verified official claims.

### Dota 2: match economy

**Map:** lane and neutral income → immediate fighting power or growth investment → objective window → death cost and buyback liquidity → closing timing.

**Player paths:** patch returner, role-specific timing plan, limited-match learner and advanced replay review.

**Pulse:** patch economy changes translated into item timing, farming and reserve decisions.

**Rankings:** economic items by payback window, reserve readiness, farm sources by risk, timing strength by match state.

**Tool stack:** Midas incremental ROI, buyback reserve, death cost, item timing comparison, farm allocation and post-match economy review.

### World of Warcraft Retail: player market economy

**Map:** gathering or inputs → craft transformation → listing cost → sell-through → liquid gold → inventory reinvestment.

**Player paths:** returning crafter, casual seller, gathering route and advanced profession portfolio.

**Pulse:** season, patch and profession-market changes with region and realm context wherever material.

**Rankings:** crafts by risk-adjusted margin, farms by effective GPH, recipes by capital lock-up, professions by time profile.

**Tool stack:** market ledger, crafting margin, farm liquidity, crafting-order commission floor, profession portfolio and supported live-price scenarios later.

### Total War: Warhammer III: campaign capital economy

**Map:** treasury and income base -> province growth -> buildings -> army upkeep -> war reserve -> conquest return -> reinvestment.

**Player paths:** campaign returner, constrained-turn stabilizer and advanced expansion optimizer.

**Pulse:** verified official patch changes translated into growth, defense, recruitment and expansion implications.

**Rankings:** choices are conditional on campaign horizon, border risk, treasury runway and the value of the next conquest.

**Tool stack:** building payback, war reserve and conquest choice. Faction presets come only after demand and data validation.

### Crusader Kings III: realm capital economy

**Map:** domain income -> holdings -> men-at-arms and court costs -> war chest -> inheritance shock -> succession recovery -> reinvestment.

**Player paths:** returning ruler, stable-dynasty builder and advanced realm allocator.

**Pulse:** current version changes plus an explicit watch layer for announced but unreleased economic systems.

**Rankings:** choices are conditional on reign horizon, control, war timing, claimant risk and expected partition pressure.

**Tool stack:** domain payback, war chest and succession buffer. Silk & Silver trade models remain blocked until release mechanics are verified.

## Quality gate for a benchmark hub

A hub reaches benchmark status only when it has:

- one original hero world and one interactive economy map;
- three real player paths with complete decisions;
- a current pulse with freshness logic;
- at least two useful rankings or comparisons;
- at least three connected decision tools or scenarios;
- at least six substantial library entries, including one flagship analysis;
- complete Russian and English parity;
- desktop/mobile visual QA, accessibility checks, source review and a branch preview.

The remaining hubs inherit the proven system, but not copied content or identical layouts.
