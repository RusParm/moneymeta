# Decision log

## 2026-08-11 — product before media

Money Meta will be built as a data-and-decision product. Articles must lead to a tool, saved result, email relationship or repeat visit.

## 2026-08-11 — static-first architecture

Use Astro and TypeScript. Avoid backend/auth complexity until local profiles and recurring utility are validated.

## 2026-08-11 — data trust architecture

Every material metric carries a verification status and check date. Stale weekly data must never be presented as current.

## 2026-08-11 — routes

Russian lives at the root; English lives under `/en/`. Existing `.html` calculator traffic receives an explicit permanent redirect to the clean route.

## 2026-08-11 — third vertical: World of Warcraft Retail

WoW Retail is the third Money Meta economy. Retail and Classic will use separate datasets and models because their markets, audiences and live data are not interchangeable. The first Retail release focuses on crafting margin and farm liquidity; live prices remain a later data-layer upgrade.
